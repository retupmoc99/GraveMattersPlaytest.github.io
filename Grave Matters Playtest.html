Playtesters wanted · Windows · free

Grave Matters, Inc.

A cemetery tycoon where the customers never really leave.

I'm making a management game about running a small churchyard, and I'm looking for a few people to play one session and tell me honestly how it went.

The game

You've taken over Hollowmere Churchyard. By day, grieving families arrive at the office, each with a budget and a wish: shade, flowers, a bench, a view of the pond. You pick the plot. Get it right and they pay extra.

By night, every resident wakes up as a ghost with a personality. Grumpy ghosts can't stand jolly neighbors, shy ones hate nosy ones, and lonely ghosts hate empty plots next door. Keep them content and they make friends, put on a show for your night tours, and eventually find peace and move on, which frees their plot to sell again. Upset them and they start feuds, chill the daytime visitors, and turn into poltergeists.

You have seven days to win over the town, put the dead at ease, and make the books balance.

What a playtest involves

  • TimeOne sitting of about 30 to 45 minutes. A full game is seven in-game days.
  • You needA 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 PC. Nothing to install: unzip the folder and run the game.
  • How to playHowever you like. Don't read up first. Seeing where people get stuck is the whole point.
  • AfterwardsSend me the short log the game writes, and a few lines on these three questions.
  1. What was your favorite moment?
  2. What confused you the most?
  3. Would you keep playing? If you got bored, when?

To find the log: press Esc in the game, click "Open playtest logs", and send the newest file.

Good to know

  • It's an early prototype. The art and sound are placeholders and there will be rough edges. Bugs you find are useful too.
  • Windows may warn you. The game isn't code-signed yet, so Windows might say it protected your PC. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
  • The log is just the game. It's a plain text file of what happened in your session: plots sold, events, money and so on. Nothing is sent automatically; you choose whether to send it.
  • It's free, with nothing to buy, and you can delete the folder to remove it.
